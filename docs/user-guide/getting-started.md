# Getting Started

This page guides you through accessing GeoDataHub for the first time, choosing an authentication method, and understanding what you'll see after logging in.

---

## Accessing the Platform

GeoDataHub is a web-based platform accessible from any modern browser. Navigate to the platform URL to reach the landing page.

!!! note
    The landing page is the only part of the platform accessible without authentication. All functionality — including viewing orders, placing requests, and managing your workspace — requires you to be logged in.

---

## Logging In

GeoDataHub supports three authentication methods. Choose the one that applies to your account:

=== ":material-bank: TaxisNet"

    **For public administration employees**

    TaxisNet is the standard Greek government authentication system. If you use TaxisNet for other public services (e.g., tax filings), you can use the same credentials here.

    1. On the landing page, click **Log in with TaxisNet**
    2. You will be redirected to the TaxisNet authentication portal
    3. Enter your TaxisNet credentials and confirm
    4. You will be returned to GeoDataHub and logged in automatically

    !!! tip
        TaxisNet login is recommended for most public sector users as it requires no separate account management.

=== ":material-satellite-variant: Axis-3 Hub"

    **For users affiliated with the governmental Axis-3 hub**

    The Axis-3 Hub is the governmental integration platform associated with the Greek microsatellite program.

    1. On the landing page, click **Log in with ACS-3**
    2. Complete authentication on the Axis-3 portal
    3. You will be returned to GeoDataHub and logged in automatically

=== ":material-account: Local Account"

    **For users with a GeoDataHub-managed account**

    If you have been issued a local account by a GeoDataHub administrator:

    1. Enter your **username** and **password** in the login form
    2. Click **Sign In**

    !!! warning "Forgot your password?"
        Contact your organization administrator or the Hellenic Space Center to reset your local account credentials.

---

## After Logging In

After successful authentication, you are redirected to your **application space** — your personal dashboard showing all satellite data orders you have submitted.

```
Landing Page → Authentication → Application Space (Orders Dashboard)
```

From here, you can:

- View all orders associated with your active workspace
- Filter and search through existing orders
- Navigate to create a new order
- Switch between workspaces

!!! info "Empty dashboard?"
    If your application space appears empty after logging in, verify that you have the correct **workspace** selected. Orders are shown per workspace, not across all workspaces at once. See [Workspaces](workspaces.md) for more information.
